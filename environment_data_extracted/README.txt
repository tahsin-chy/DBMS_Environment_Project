ENVIRONMENTAL DATASETS
======================

This folder contains the environmental datasets used in the
Environmental Database for Bangladesh project.

The datasets cover different environmental and geographical domains,
including:

- Geographical information
- Climate and weather
- Rainfall and precipitation
- Flood and disaster information
- River and sediment data
- Land use
- Environmental indicators
- Arsenic contamination
- Greenhouse gas emissions
- City-level and source-level emissions

Data Processing:
The raw datasets are examined, cleaned, standardized, and transformed
before being loaded into the MySQL database through the ETL scripts.

File Organization:
Datasets are organized according to their respective environmental
domains or database tables where applicable.

Note:
The datasets are used for academic and research purposes within this
project. Original data sources should be acknowledged when the data
is used or redistributed.
